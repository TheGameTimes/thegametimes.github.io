GTA 6 Is Getting an Extended Look on August 27

Rockstar Games has announced an extended look at Grand Theft Auto VI, giving fans another major glimpse at the highly anticipated game ahead of its November release.

By The Game Times • August 11, 2026 • 3 MIN READ
GTA VI HERO IMAGE

Rockstar Games is giving Grand Theft Auto VI another major spotlight. The developer has announced an “Extended Look” at the game for August 27 at 3 PM ET, according to the official Grand Theft Auto VI website.

The presentation arrives ahead of GTA VI's currently scheduled November 19, 2026 release, with the game set to launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Another Look at Vice City

Rockstar has already shown fans its new version of Vice City, alongside protagonists Jason and Lucia and the wider criminal world of Leonida.

The upcoming extended look gives Rockstar another opportunity to show how the world, characters and gameplay systems come together before launch.

“An Extended Look” — Rockstar Games

When Is the GTA 6 Extended Look?

Rockstar has scheduled the extended look for August 27 at 3 PM ET.

With the release date currently set for November 19, this could become one of the biggest official GTA VI showcases before the game finally reaches players.

The Game Times' Take

GTA VI is now entering the part of its marketing cycle where every official reveal carries serious weight. Rockstar doesn't need to flood fans with information — it just needs to show enough of the game to make the wait until November feel even longer.

And judging by the level of anticipation surrounding GTA VI, Rockstar probably doesn't have to do much convincing.
