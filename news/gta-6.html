GTA 6 Is Getting an Extended Look on August 27

Rockstar Games is preparing another major look at Grand Theft Auto VI ahead of its November release.

AUGUST 11, 2026 • THE GAME TIMES

Rockstar Games is preparing to give Grand Theft Auto VI another major spotlight, with an extended look reportedly arriving on August 27.

The upcoming reveal comes as anticipation continues to build around Rockstar's next open-world blockbuster, which is currently scheduled to launch in November.

Another Look at Vice City

Grand Theft Auto VI is set to return players to Vice City, introducing a new story, characters and a much larger world for players to explore.

Rockstar has remained relatively selective about what it shows publicly, making every new trailer, screenshot and announcement a major event for the gaming community.

“The next major look at GTA VI could reveal more about Rockstar's vision for the game.”

What Happens Next?

With the release approaching, Rockstar is expected to gradually reveal more information about the game, including additional gameplay details and features.
